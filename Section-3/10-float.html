10 — float (legacy but still useful for text wrap)

✅ Classic use: text wrapping around an image

IMAGE

This text flows AROUND the floated image. Float was originally designed for this exact thing — magazine-style layouts. The image is pushed to the left and the text fills in the space to the right, then wraps below it when it runs out. Even in modern CSS, this is still the easiest way to wrap text around an image. Note the margin on the image creates breathing room around it. This is the ONLY real-world use case you should reach for float in 2026 — for everything else, use Flexbox or Grid.

Float right (same thing, other side)

IMAGE

Now the image is floated to the right, and the text flows along its left side. This is how most blog posts / magazines structure their content. Notice how naturally the text flows around the floated element, including wrapping under it once the bottom of the image is reached.
