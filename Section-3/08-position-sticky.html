08 — position: sticky

Scroll slowly! Each section's header sticks to the top as you scroll through it, then the next one pushes it up.

🍎 Section 1 — Fruits

Apples, bananas, cherries, dates. Scroll down slowly and watch this pink header stick to the top! Once you scroll past this section, the next header will push it away and take its place. That's the magic of position: sticky.

🥦 Section 2 — Vegetables

Broccoli, carrots, eggplant, kale. The blue header is now sticky. It behaves like a regular element until you scroll past its normal position, then it STICKS to the top.

🍗 Section 3 — Proteins

Chicken, fish, tofu, beans. Keep scrolling!

🍞 Section 4 — Grains

Rice, oats, wheat, quinoa. One more section to go!

🍰 Section 5 — Desserts

Cake, pie, cookies, ice cream. And we're done!

The CSS

.sticky-header {
  position: sticky;
  top: 0;           /* stick when scroll hits top */
}

💡 Important: sticky only works inside its nearest scrolling parent. If a parent has overflow: hidden, sticky may not work!
